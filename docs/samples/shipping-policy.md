# Shipping and Delivery — FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY

This is fabricated training content for ResolveOps, not real company guidance.

## Processing
Orders normally take 1 to 2 business days to process before shipment. Peak sale periods can cause delays.

## Tracking
After shipment, customers receive a tracking number by email. Support agents can help obtain delivery details. This public policy document contains no private or real-time order status.

## Missing packages
If tracking says delivered but a package is missing, check delivery locations and neighbors and open a support ticket. A human support employee investigates with the shipping carrier; a refund is not automatically guaranteed.

## International shipping
International delivery may be delayed by local customs inspections and duties. Published delivery estimates do not include customs delays.
