# Returns and Refunds — FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY

This document is fabricated for ResolveOps demonstrations; it is not a real business policy.

## Return window
Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery is marked completed. Returns after 30 days require a manager's manual review. Include all original accessories.

## Damaged items
For damaged products, contact customer support within 7 days of delivery with clear photos of the packaging and item. An agent opens a review ticket.

## Refund handling
After an approved return is received, refunds usually process to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. The AI assistant cannot issue or approve refunds; human authorization is required.

## Exclusions
Digital gift cards and custom-engraved goods cannot normally be returned unless applicable law requires otherwise.
